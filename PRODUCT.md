# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, potential collaborators, graduate-program reviewers, and machine-learning employers assessing Safwan's research interests, publications, engineering experience, and professional credibility.

## Product Purpose

Present Safwan Islam Nabeel as a rigorous machine-learning researcher and engineer, make his published work and credentials easy to verify, and give relevant visitors clear paths to read his research, review his CV, inspect his code, or contact him.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, and understated. The site should feel intellectually serious, precise, and approachable without adopting the visual language of a startup pitch deck.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy startup visuals, exaggerated claims, decorative technology motifs, excessive animation, and generic card-heavy portfolio templates. Do not make ongoing or under-review work appear published.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with verifiable work rather than broad claims.
2. Make research, experience, and contact paths effortless to scan.
3. Use concise, technically credible language that remains understandable outside a narrow specialty.
4. Let one strong portrait and disciplined typography create personality.
5. Clearly distinguish published, ongoing, and under-review work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, useful alternative text, comfortable reading widths, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
