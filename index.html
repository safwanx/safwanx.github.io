01 / About

Research that survives contact with reality.

My interests sit at the intersection of multimodal learning, computer vision, privacy, explainability, and robustness. I am especially interested in failure modes that are easy to miss when evaluation is reduced to a single benchmark score.

I enjoy research problems where the assumptions themselves need to be questioned: what information a model is really using, whether an explanation is actually meaningful, and whether a system remains trustworthy under domain shift or deployment constraints.

02 / Research

What I care about

Broad enough to cross domains, focused enough to keep one question at the center: can we trust what the model is doing?

01

Trustworthy multimodal AI

Multimodal systems that combine vision, language, and structured information while remaining transparent about uncertainty and failure.

02

Privacy in visual systems

Studying what sensitive information survives common anonymization strategies and how privacy claims should actually be evaluated.

03

Reliability under shift

Understanding how models behave when the people, environments, sensors, or distributions at deployment differ from training.

04

Human-verifiable AI

Moving beyond explanations that merely look plausible toward evidence and reasoning that a human can meaningfully inspect.
